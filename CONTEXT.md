# Dream Foundry

Human-directed exploration that turns research and experiments into supported decisions and, eventually, executable art.

## Work and responsibility

**Campaign**:
A collection of related Work Orders pursuing a stated outcome within a human-authorized allowance.
_Avoid_: Session, process

**Work Order**:
A scoped request with a desired outcome, acceptance criteria, permitted activity, and a stopping point. Research and artwork production are both kinds of Work Order.
_Avoid_: Prompt, ticket

**Trial**:
A particular attempt to answer a Work Order through a stated hypothesis or method. A Work Order may have several Trials.
_Avoid_: Iteration, agent turn

**Assignment**:
A time-bounded grant for a named worker to perform a Trial within a stated scope.
_Avoid_: Ownership forever, claim comment

**Submission**:
A fixed collection of outputs offered for assessment, such as a report, an experiment result, a proposed change, or an artwork Candidate.
_Avoid_: Latest files, finished work

**Evidence**:
Recorded observations and measurements associated with a specified Submission or Trial.
_Avoid_: Progress claim, confidence

**Inspection Receipt**:
A fixed account of which source was inspected under which rules and what was observed. It is a form of Evidence, not an Assessment or Acceptance.
_Avoid_: Gate verdict, certification

**Assessment**:
A judgment of specified Evidence against a particular Work Order and its criteria.
_Avoid_: Score, approval

**Acceptance**:
A decision that a particular Submission satisfies the stated Work Order criteria. Acceptance is distinct from permission to merge, publish, or deploy it.
_Avoid_: Merge, release

**Operating Allowance**:
The human-authorized limits on a Campaign's activity, including its permitted work and resource use.
_Avoid_: Subscription capacity, unlimited resources

## Creative exploration

**Dream**:
A coherent creative target within a Campaign, including intended appearance, behavior, and qualities that must be preserved.
_Avoid_: Prompt, target image

**Candidate**:
A fixed realization of a Dream that can be compared with other realizations. Research reports are Submissions, not artwork Candidates.
_Avoid_: Branch, agent session

**Repertoire**:
A retained collection of distinct Candidates whose useful qualities are supported by Evidence.
_Avoid_: Leaderboard, latest best

**Promotion**:
The decision to retain a Candidate in the Repertoire. An accepted research Submission is not thereby a promoted artwork.
_Avoid_: Acceptance, merge
