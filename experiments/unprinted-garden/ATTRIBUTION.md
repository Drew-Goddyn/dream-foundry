# Artwork and dependency attribution

Dream Foundry owns the original press, ink creature, botanical drawings, living mark, river and bank geometry, composition, score, interaction history, integration command and delivery. INKBORN's original creature rig is retained and materially repainted for this campaign.

The rendering and musical dependency is [alexgreensh/anidoodle](https://github.com/alexgreensh/anidoodle/tree/03ddf534328962f8a91eb115e3ae67e03da4de5a), revision `03ddf534328962f8a91eb115e3ae67e03da4de5a`, under Apache-2.0. The repository's [license](../../third_party/anidoodle/LICENSE) and [notice](../../third_party/anidoodle/NOTICE) accompany every local build.

This piece directly invokes the pinned `canvas-core/core.ts` material operations and `bake.ts` cropped-part path through existing local build tools. It uses the actual pressure ribbon, displaced wet-edge wash, pigment rim, paper/tooth, layer composition and pooling machinery. The paper reserve, selective contour and lower-edge pigment concentration apply the technique demonstrated by `wren.ts` to original Motor-evaluated creature geometry. The steel and brass members use ordered engraving rather than coating every surface with wash. Drawing geometry is original; the wren, watch and lighthouse subjects are comparison plates only.

The original motif is performed and synthesized by anidoodle's `music/plan.ts`, `perform.ts`, `render.ts` and their existing procedural instrument/DSP dependencies. No recordings, weights, generated neural assets or third-party songs are used. Measurements establish technical audio properties, not an audition.

Motor is an existing authorized external installation. Foundry uses documented parsing, compilation and detached pose evaluation. Its source, runtime bundles, internal documentation and generated packages remain local and outside tracked source. This integration changes no Motor repository or active Motor worktree.
