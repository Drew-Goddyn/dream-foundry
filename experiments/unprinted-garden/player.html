How to make an impression

Draw one line on the paper with the vermilion nib. Lift your pointer to finish it, then print. The press keeps your line; the garden grows from its bends. You can pause its creation and continue from that moment. Your world stays until you reset.

Keyboard: focus the drawing, move with the arrow keys, and press Space to begin or finish your stroke. Enter prints; Escape pauses or continues; R resets. Shift + arrow makes a larger step. The guided performance follows the same drawing and printing rules. Touching the scene takes over the guide.

Sound is off until you enable it. Save plate downloads the painted view; Save my replay preserves the drawing and every action so you can open it here again.

Original world and score by Dream Foundry. Material rendering and synthesis use anidoodle. Study the plates.
