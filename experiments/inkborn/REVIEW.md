1. Read the assigned brief and [usage](README.md). Record the Foundry commit plus

   weak, usable with a material weakness, convincing, exceptional. Weights are
   creature acting 20%, bracing/tail/force path 25%, press-to-botanical continuity
   25%, ink/material craft 20%, composition 10%. Obvious disconnections or an
   already-botanical opening cap force and transformation at 1. Name the exact
   pose and visible consequence behind any requested repair.
5. Record PASS, FAIL or BLOCKED separately for runnable behavior, visual rubric,
   observed motion, dependency use and public-source privacy. A still-image

   browser observations. Stop with a useful candidate judgment or a concrete
   blocker; acceptance, merges and deployment remain the user's decisions.
