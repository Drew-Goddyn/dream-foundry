# Fast-path planning revision

The human interrupted the foundation work to request faster progress and no long experiments. This revision changes the plan rather than merely adding a conflicting note to it.

## Superseded

The separate preflight launch, mandatory transport/protocol research lanes, full eighteen-case offline simulator, and documentation-only acceptance gate before useful implementation are removed as prerequisites. Work Order #6 becomes the first real local workboard build. #1 and #4 are folded into its startup and short tests; #3 is optional blocker research; #5 reviews the implemented candidate.

## Preserved

The goal remains a small local CPU-only precursor swarm for research, documentation, experimentation, and planning, eventually supporting executable art. Human-controlled launches, isolated writers, fixed evidence, fresh review, no assumed GPU/cloud spend, and explicit acceptance/merge remain intact.

## Current scope versus future guarantees

The first deliverable is a supervised CLI workboard, not autonomous dispatch. Basic claim collision, evidence integrity, review bookkeeping, reopen, and cancellation are tested now. Automatic expiry/reassignment, unattended restart, process supervision, and remote-write recovery are tested when those features are introduced. Those guarantees have not been silently certified or abandoned.

This is an author revision, not independent assessment. Historical CI in foundation-self-check.md does not establish that this new revision passes; inspect its own current PR checks. No local builder or reviewer is launched by publishing the revision.
